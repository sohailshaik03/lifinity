NEW TABLES (6 total, supporting new features)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
1. products
   - SKU, name, category, cost_price, selling_price
   - current_stock, timestamps
   - UNIQUE constraint on (shop_id, sku)

2. expiry_records
   - Batch-level tracking (batch_number, qty_received, qty_remaining)
   - Expiry date and calculated days_left (computed column)
   - Status tracking (active, expiring, expired)

3. waste_records
   - Link to product and expiry_record
   - qty_wasted, reason, recorded_by (user)
   - Audit trail with timestamp

4. discount_rules
   - Per-shop rules for dynamic pricing
   - Days_left range (min/max), qty_min, discount_percent
   - Active flag for enabling/disabling rules

5. alert_notifications
   - Alert delivery tracking
   - recipient_email and recipient_phone
   - sent status, delivery_status, sent_at timestamp
   - INDEX on (shop_id, sent) for performance

6. alert_settings
   - Per-shop alert configuration
   - email_enabled, sms_enabled toggles
   - alert_days_threshold (1-30 days)
   - alert_emails, alert_phones (JSON arrays)
